One-time step · for parents

Parent or guardian consent

Before your child starts practising, a parent or guardian needs to agree. We send an OTP to the parent's WhatsApp to confirm.

I am the parent or guardian of this student. I allow SIR, Kolhapur to use my child's name, school, class, district/taluka and practice results in the SAGE programme:
  • to run practice missions and show my child's progress, streaks and scores;
  • to show my child on SAGE leaderboards using only first name, initial, school and class — never the phone number;
  • to contact me on WhatsApp about SAGE and the scholarship.
SIR will not sell this data or show ads to my child. I can withdraw consent at any time by contacting SIR; my child's SAGE practice data will then be deleted.
