What if a fort was actually a science problem?

Don't look at a fort only as something from the past. Look at it like a scientist. Why this height? Why this shape? Why this wall thickness? Why here? Why this structure?

The moment you start asking “why?” you have already started thinking like a scientist.

The Science Behind the Question

A real-world problem can be understood by observing it, measuring it, finding the science behind it, and designing a solution. That's how curiosity turns into understanding.
