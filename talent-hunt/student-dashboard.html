About SAGE

Scholarship for Academic Growth & Excellence.

SAGE — Scholarship For Academic Growth and Excellence is a multidisciplinary aptitude examination for Class X students. It is built around concepts generally encountered from Classes VI–X, with emphasis on conceptual understanding, application, reasoning and problem-solving rather than memorisation.

SAGE • Understand. Apply. Reason. Solve.

Your potential deserves more than a score.

SAGE gives you a chance to explore different ways of thinking, challenge yourself with unfamiliar situations and understand what you can do with what you know.
